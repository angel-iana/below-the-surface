# Below the Surface

**Romanian infrastructure in public data.**

An open, verifiable record of how Romania's roads, bridges, railways, energy networks and industrial plants are inspected and maintained, built only from public sources.

**Live page:** https://angel-iana.github.io/below-the-surface/

## Status
In development. Data is being collected and checked; no figures are published yet. The current state of each step and sector is in the *Progress* section of the live page.

## Commitments
- **Verifiable**: every fact leads back to its document, page and exact sentence.
- **Quality-checked**: every fact has a stated confidence level; unverified or conflicting facts are never published.
- **Useful**: built to support decisions by asset owners, inspection companies and the public.

## Principles
1. **Truth**: only what a source actually says.
2. **Transparency**: sources, methods, confidence levels, limits and corrections are shown openly.
3. **Open data**: official open data and public documents, used within their licences and the law.

The full rules and their legal basis are in the *Terms and conditions* section of the live page.

## What this repository contains
Only the public presentation layer: the project page and, later, published dashboards built from checked, aggregated data. The data pipeline and source documents are not part of this repository.
